## **"Favorite Scene"**

**Captain America stands alone against Thanos massive army, hears "On your left"from Sam Wilson (Falcon), and watches as mystical portals open up across the ruined battlefield to return all the fallen heroes before shouting, "Avengers... assemble!".**

**Iron Man's Sacrifice ("I am Iron Man"): Tony Stark makes the ultimate sacrifice by snatching the Infinity Stones from Thanos and snapping his fingers to save the universe, echoing his iconic line from the 2008 film that started the MCU.**

**During the fierce three-on-one fight against Thanos, Thor is disarmed, and Mjolnir flies right into Steve Rogers's hand, proving his worthiness to the astonishment of Thor and the thrill of the audience.**
